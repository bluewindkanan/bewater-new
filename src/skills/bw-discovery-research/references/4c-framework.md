# 4C research framework

4C is a coverage compass — four base questions to keep research from being lopsided — and nothing
more. Begin with the current Charter revision and active root assumptions; the questions below are
prompts for research, not prefilled answers. A blind spot checked here becomes an agenda entry only
when it is material; no lens, base or extended, is a mandatory task.

## Base lenses — the 4C blind-spot compass

These four lenses are the canonical blind-spot check for any Discover project:

| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| **Consumer** | Who is the user or buyer, and why do they switch, struggle, or stay? |
| **Company** | What do we already have — assets, constraints, and commitments? |
| **Category** | Which arena are we playing in, and how is it structured? |
| **Channel** | What channel do we reach the user through, and what does it cost? |

## Extended lenses — enter only when material

Some challenges carry a blind spot the base four cannot see. Add an extended lens to the agenda only
when the Charter's innovation challenge or a strategic uncertainty makes it material. A lens never implies a task, report chapter, method, or worker; it is a coverage compass, not a work order.

- **Technology** — which technology shifts could change what is possible or economic?
- **Regulation** — which rules, standards, or pending changes shape what may be done?
- **Economics** — which unit economics or cost/profit structure decides who wins?
- **Ecosystem** — which actors, intermediaries, or platform dynamics reshape reach and value?
- **Future** — which trends, weak signals, or structural shifts change the future choice territory?

## Using the compass

For each material lens, record the question, priority, coverage status (`planned`, `evidenced`, or
`gap-accepted`), and the evidence needed. A gap may be accepted only with a stated limitation and
reason; there is no fixed fact quota per lens. Use public sources and any supplied context as
appropriate. An observation remains a hypothesis until evidence supports it. The Initial Assessment
can supply candidate beliefs to challenge, but it never supplies a Fact or evidence by itself.

A lens is a coverage compass, not a research sequence, a report structure, a method assignment, or a
worker boundary. The four base lenses must be checked for strategy-changing blind spots; the extended
lenses are conditional, not a checklist to exhaust.
