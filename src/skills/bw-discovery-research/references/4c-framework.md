# 4C research framework

4C is a navigation compass — four questions to answer so research is not lopsided. Begin with the
current Charter revision and active root assumptions; the starting questions below are prompts for
research, not prefilled answers.

| C | Question | Research type |
|---|---|---|
| **Consumer** | Who is buying, and why? | Design research (empathy) |
| **Company** | What do we already have? | Stakeholder interviews |
| **Category** | Which arena are we playing in? | Market research |
| **Channel** | What channel do we go through? | Market research |

For each C, record the question, priority, coverage status (`planned`, `evidenced`, or
`gap-accepted`), and the evidence needed. A gap may be accepted only with a stated limitation and
reason; no C has a fixed fact quota. Use online sources and supplied context as appropriate. An
observation remains a hypothesis until evidence supports it. The Initial Assessment can supply
candidate beliefs to challenge, but never supplies a Fact or evidence by itself.

## Initialization output

The research artifact begins with the Charter's **core exploration proposition**, the **four 4C questions**, the active root assumptions, and the living **Discover Plan**. These are research
prompts and priorities, not findings.
