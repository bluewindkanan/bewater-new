# Root-assumption projection

Research Planning may project zero qualifying root assumptions; there is no quota. Project only a
starting belief whose failure could materially change direction, is uncertain and falsifiable, and
has an observable disconfirming signal.

Allocate a new A-ID from `ledger.next_id`. Its `derived_from` contains the exact Research Plan revision that introduced
the root. The Plan may show `ledger_ref`, but assumption refs never enter its
`derived_from`, which contains the exact Charter revision only.

`impact=high` and `uncertainty=high` immediately derives `is_achilles_heel` and opens the existing
durable L4 obligation. Research Planning does not replace Define's complete G1 inventory review.

Pre-existing Charter-derived roots are grandfathered. Updates retain exact Charter provenance and
must never reparent the same A-ID. Materially changed beliefs get new IDs; wording-only edits do not.
