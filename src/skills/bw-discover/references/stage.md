# Discover stage

Discover uses 4C-directed exploration and field methods to refine sourced facts into candidate
insights. It exits with a human-signed Insight Portfolio, not directional hypotheses.

## Formal inputs

The formal Discover inputs are the selected branch's current Charter revision plus its active root
assumptions. The Charter supplies the question, knowledge state, research map, and boundary.

An Initial Assessment is optional advisory context. Consume it only when its branch and exact
`derived_from` snapshot match the formal inputs. Map Candidate Insights to candidate judgments to
validate, Core Conflict / Tension to the priority challenge, Most Promising Direction to a candidate
research path, and Key Risks to disconfirming questions. Never promote these directly to Fact,
Evidence, Accepted Belief, or F/P/E/T Insight.

Ignore stale, cross-branch, and snapshot-mismatched reports. State the advisory gap, but do not block
Discover when the formal inputs exist.

## Capabilities to route to

- **bw-discovery-research** — initialize and iterate the Discover Plan, then run Consumer, Company,
  Category, and Channel research Sprints.
- **bw-insight-craft** — generate and judge candidate insights against F/P/E/T.

If a formal input is missing, route back to `bw-project-charter`; this stage does not create a Discover Brief.
Keep Fact, Evidence, Accepted Belief, Insight, and Hypothesis distinct.

## Exit criteria

Insights pass F/P/E/T; Fact, Accepted Belief, and Insight remain distinguishable; 4C coverage and
accepted gaps are visible; and at least one insight is surprising but plausible. Hand the signed
Insight Portfolio to Define, where directional hypotheses are composed.
