# Home Bartender Interview Notes

- Three interviewees said they kept their subscription because the box made drinks they would not otherwise try; two of the three mentioned missing ingredient flexibility for basic staples like vodka.
- Two interviewees valued receiving a recipe card and a reusable branded jigger; both said the ritual of making one new drink per week justified the cost.
- One interviewee cancelled after two boxes because the flavor profiles repeated and the remaining bottles crowded their shelf.
- Interviewees were recruited from the organizer's existing email list, so heavy hobbyists are likely overrepresented.
