# Internal Report: Subscription Box Unit Economics (Q2)

- Gross margin per box averages 31% before packaging; the two most expensive ingredients in the current discovery-kit mix account for most of the COGS variance.
- Month-3 retention across the cohort was 41%, and churn concentrated in weeks 8-10, the point where the first repeat flavor profiles appeared.
- Our internal benchmark set 55% month-3 retention as the target for a renewal, but that figure was set by the founding team and has not been revalidated externally.
- Marketing reports that acquisition relies on paid social; organic signup share is not currently tracked.
