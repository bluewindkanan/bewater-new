# Charter

Freelance chefs need a faster way to find compliant pop-up kitchens for specific events, while
kitchen operators may benefit from monetizing suitable off-peak capacity. The first cycle examines
one municipality and does not optimize a nationwide marketplace. Unknowns include how often access
blocks paid work, whether supply overlaps demand windows, and how much regulatory variation a
matching layer can absorb.
