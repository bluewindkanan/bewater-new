# Charter

Freelance chefs need a faster way to find compliant pop-up kitchens for specific events, while
kitchen operators may benefit from monetizing suitable off-peak capacity. The revised first cycle
examines two neighboring municipalities and still excludes a nationwide marketplace. Unknowns
include how often access blocks paid work, whether supply overlaps demand windows, and how local
regulatory differences affect a matching layer.
